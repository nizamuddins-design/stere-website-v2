Information & Security

How Stere protects its systems, its people and our customers’ data.

Last updated May 10, 2026 SOC 2 Type II ISO 27001:2022

Contents

1Overview 2Purpose 3Scope 4Certifications and assurance 5Security incident reporting 6Device security 7Remote access 8Acceptable use 9Policy compliance 10Violations and enforcement
1

Overview

This Information Security Policy sets out how Stere protects its people, its partners and its customers’ data from illegal or damaging actions, whether intentional or accidental.

Stere’s systems — computing equipment, software, operating systems, storage, and network accounts providing email, web access and file transfer — are company property, provided for business purposes in serving the interests of Stere and our customers.

Effective security is a team effort. Every Stere employee and contractor who handles information or information systems is responsible for understanding this policy and working in line with it.

2

Purpose

This policy communicates our information security principles and sets out the acceptable use and protection of Stere’s information and assets. These rules exist to protect our customers, our employees and Stere itself. Inappropriate use exposes the company to risks including malware, compromise of network systems and services, and legal and compliance exposure.

3

Scope

This policy applies to the use of information, electronic and computing devices, and network resources used to conduct Stere business or to interact with our internal networks and business systems — whether owned or leased by Stere, by an individual, or by a third party.

It applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff and other workers at Stere and its subsidiaries, including personnel affiliated with third parties, and to all Stere-controlled company and customer data as well as all equipment, systems, networks and software owned or leased by Stere.

4

Certifications and assurance

Stere maintains an information security management system certified to recognized international standards, and is independently audited on an annual basis.

SOC 2 Type II Security, availability and confidentiality, audited annually.
ISO 27001:2022 Certified information security management system.

Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and reviewed regularly, and multi-factor authentication is enforced across our systems. To request our current reports or discuss our security program, contact security@stere.io.

5

Security incident reporting

Everyone is required to report known or suspected security events and incidents, including policy violations and observed weaknesses. Report immediately, or as soon as possible, by email — describing the incident or observation along with any relevant details.

Report a security concern: security@stere.io

6

Device security

All end-user devices — phones, tablets, laptops and desktops — must comply with this policy, whether company-owned or personal devices used for work. Take particular care with email attachments from unknown senders.

  • Devices must be encrypted and locked automatically after a short period of inactivity, and locked whenever left unattended.
  • Suspected misuse, loss or theft of a device must be reported immediately.
  • Confidential information must not be stored on personal devices or removable media.
  • Devices used to access company systems must not be shared with anyone else.
  • On termination, company devices must be returned and all company information and accounts removed from personal devices.

System- and user-level passwords must meet our access control standards. Sharing access with another person, deliberately or by failing to secure it, is prohibited.

7

Remote access

Devices used to reach the Stere network must meet the security requirements set out in this policy:

  • Connect only with an up-to-date software firewall in place, and never disable required security controls such as firewalls or endpoint protection.
  • Use only company-provided remote access services, configured with multi-factor authentication. Unauthorized remote access tools may not be installed on any Stere system.
  • Use encrypted connections when transmitting confidential information over public networks.
  • Avoid public or shared computers. If unavoidable, do not save credentials, log out of the session, and delete any downloaded files.
8

Acceptable use

Stere proprietary and customer information stored on any device — company, personal or third-party — remains the property of Stere for the purposes of this policy. Employees and contractors must protect that information through appropriate legal and technical means, and must store business documents in approved company systems rather than only locally.

You are responsible for promptly reporting theft, loss or unauthorized disclosure of Stere proprietary information, and may access, use or share it only as authorized and necessary to do your job. For security and network maintenance purposes, authorized personnel may monitor equipment, systems and network traffic at any time, and Stere may audit networks and systems periodically to verify compliance.

Unacceptable use

No one at Stere is authorized to engage in activity that is illegal under local, state, federal or international law while using company resources. The following are prohibited without exception. This list is not exhaustive; employees may be exempted only where a restriction conflicts with their legitimate job responsibilities.

  • Infringing copyright, trade secret, patent or other intellectual property rights — including installing or distributing unlicensed software.
  • Accessing data, servers or accounts for any purpose other than conducting Stere business, even where you have authorized access.
  • Exporting software, technical information or encryption technology in violation of export control laws.
  • Introducing malicious code into any network or system.
  • Sharing your password or allowing anyone else to use your account.
  • Using company assets to procure or transmit material that violates harassment or hostile-workplace laws.
  • Making fraudulent offers, or statements of warranty outside your normal job duties, from a Stere account.
  • Causing security breaches or disruption of network communication — including accessing data you are not the intended recipient of, unauthorized scanning, network monitoring, circumventing authentication, or denial-of-service activity.
  • Disclosing information about Stere employees, contractors, partners or customers outside the company without authorization.

Email and communications

When using company resources online, you represent Stere and should act accordingly. Sending unsolicited bulk or advertising email, harassment of any kind, forging email headers, harvesting addresses, and chain or pyramid schemes are prohibited without exception.

9

Policy compliance

Stere measures and verifies compliance with this policy through a range of methods, including business tool reporting and both internal and external audits.

10

Violations and enforcement

Known violations of this policy should be reported to the Head of Security. Violations can result in immediate withdrawal or suspension of system and network privileges, and disciplinary action in accordance with company procedures up to and including termination of employment.

To report a vulnerability, request our SOC 2 report, or review our security program with your team, get in touch.
