Company · About

We sell strategic freedom, not just software.

Stere exists to liberate insurers and MGAs from the constraints of legacy technology — fusing deep underwriting expertise with high-scale software engineering, so the industry can move at the speed of software.

The manifesto

For decades, the pace of insurance innovation has been dictated by the limits of legacy systems. Great products died in year-long build queues. Whole markets went unserved because the core couldn’t support them.

We built Stere to end that. Our platform is a layer of freedom on top of the systems insurers already run — letting carriers, MGAs, mutuals, and distribution platforms launch, underwrite, and distribute at the speed of software, without the existential risk of a rip-and-replace. We don’t just ship features; we give our customers back the one thing legacy took from them: the ability to move.

The fusion

Two disciplines, one platform.

Deep underwriting expertise

We understand risk, rating, and regulation because we come from the industry. The platform encodes real underwriting discipline — not a generic workflow tool dressed up for insurance.

High-scale software engineering

We build like a modern software company — API-first, secure by design, and fast. The result is infrastructure that behaves like the best developer platforms, applied to the hardest problems in insurance.

Security & trust

Built to meet the strictest compliance standards.

We hold ourselves to the standards of the world’s largest financial institutions. Fully certified with SOC 2 and ISO 27001:2022, security is engineered in — never bolted on.
