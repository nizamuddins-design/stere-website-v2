1

Introduction

Stere, Inc. and its affiliates and subsidiaries (“Stere”, “we” or “us”) provide an insurance Platform-as-a-Service that enables carriers, MGAs, mutuals and distribution platforms (our “Clients”) to build, underwrite, distribute and administer insurance products through our web applications, portals and APIs (the “Platform”). In delivering the Platform, we process information about the individuals who interact with our Clients’ programs — brokers, agents, applicants and policyholders (“Users”).

This Policy is incorporated by reference into the Stere Site Terms of Use and the Stere Master Agreement. Terms not defined here have the meanings given in those documents.

2

Purpose and scope

This Policy explains how we use and protect personal information collected through the “Services” — our websites, and our products and services, including the Stere Platform as defined in the Master Agreement.

This Policy covers only information collected by us in the course of our business, including through the Services and with respect to the people we employ and manage. It does not apply to other websites, products or services that may be linked to or available through the Services, nor to the practices of companies we do not control or people we do not employ or manage.

Everyone whose responsibilities include processing personal information on behalf of Stere is expected to protect that data by adhering to this Policy. This Policy is intended to meet requirements globally, including in North America, Europe, APAC and other jurisdictions.

3

Information we collect and use

The types of personal information we collect — directly from you or from third-party sources — and our privacy practices depend on the nature of your relationship with Stere and the requirements of applicable law. We endeavour to collect only information that is relevant for the purposes of processing.

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • Communications with us. Your name, email address, phone number, company and role when you request a demo, ask about the Platform, request customer or technical support, or otherwise contact us.
  • Account registration. Credentials and profile details when you or your organization register for access to the Platform.
  • Surveys and research. Responses you voluntarily provide when we ask for feedback on the Platform. Participation is always optional.

3.2 Information collected automatically

As you navigate the Services, we may collect your IP address, cookie identifiers, browser type and language, device and operating system information, approximate geo-location derived from IP, referring and exit pages, the date and time of your visit, pages viewed and links clicked, and other actions taken through the Services.

3.3 Information about Clients

  • Name and identity, email address, business address and other contact information, professional information, log-in data, and billing information.
  • Transactional information based on your activities on the Platform, and information set out in the applicable Order or SOW.
  • Correspondence sent to us, including support tickets and dispute resolution records.
  • Sign-on data, page-view statistics, IP address and standard web log information.
  • Supplemental information we may request where information previously provided cannot be verified.

3.4 Information about Users

Stere may have access to personal information about Users (“User Data”) in the course of providing the Platform to a Client — for example, information contained in a submission, quote, policy or claim that a Client processes on Stere.

We treat User Data as confidential and do not use it for any purpose other than providing the Services to our Clients, or as instructed by the Client and permitted by applicable law.

In most cases Stere receives User Data only from the Client and never interacts with the User directly. Where a Client configures the Platform to surface a portal or notification to a User, that interaction remains under the Client’s control. Stere accesses User Data only as instructed by the Client and only to perform Services on the Client’s behalf. If a User contacts Stere directly, we collect personal information only as necessary to respond and to direct the User to their Client, and we then delete or anonymize that data.

3.5 Information from other sources

We may receive information about you from other sources to supplement what you provide — for example business contact and firmographic data from professional or industry data providers, or information from a Client that identifies you as an authorized contact. This helps us verify information you have given us and improve how we communicate with you.

4

How we use personal information

We acquire, hold, use and process personal information for a range of business purposes:

To provide the Services or information requested. Manage accounts, respond to questions and requests, provide access to areas and features of the Platform, deliver customer and technical support, and register you for events.

Administrative purposes. Measure interest in our Services, develop new products and features, ensure internal quality control, verify identity, communicate about accounts and changes to our policies, send operational email, process payments and applications, prevent prohibited or illegal activity, and enforce our terms.

Marketing our products and services. Tailor content and offers, and notify you about products, services and events that may be of interest, by email, phone or post as permitted by applicable law. You may opt out at any time — see Section 6.

Research and development. Create non-identifiable information, alone or aggregated with other sources, to improve existing products and develop new ones.

Security and integrity. Detect, investigate and prevent unauthorized access, fraud and abuse of the Platform, and maintain audit and access logs.

Anonymized and aggregated information. Create de-identified and aggregated datasets — for example usage analytics — which are not personal information and which we may use for research, internal analysis and any other lawful purpose.

Legal and compliance. Meet our regulatory, insurance-related and contractual obligations, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

5

Cookies and analytics

We and selected third parties that provide functionality on our Services use cookies, pixel tags, local storage and similar technologies (“Technologies”) to collect information automatically as you use the Services.

  • Cookies. Small text files placed in your browser to store preferences and keep you signed in. Most browsers let you block and delete cookies, though parts of the Services may then not work properly.
  • Pixel tags and web beacons. Code embedded in a page or email that records, for example, that a page was viewed or a link was clicked.
  • Analytics. We use web analytics tools to understand visitor behaviour and improve our content. This analytics data is not tied to policy or submission data on the Platform.

Our use of these Technologies falls into three categories: operationally necessary (access, security and irregular-behaviour detection), performance related (understanding how the Services are used), and functionality related (remembering your preferences). You can browse our marketing site without telling us who you are; if you would like to opt out of these Technologies, you may block, delete or disable them as your browser or device permits.

6

Third-party websites and services

The Services may contain links to other websites, and other websites may link to ours. Those domains are not controlled by us, and Stere makes no representations about them. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website and application you interact with. Visiting them is at your own risk.

Our marketing pages may include features from professional social platforms such as LinkedIn. If you already use those platforms, their cookies may be set on your device when you visit our site, and your interactions with those features are governed by that provider’s privacy policy.

Payment processing

Fees for the Platform are invoiced under an Order or SOW. Where payment is made through a third-party payment processor, that processor may collect a Client’s name, email address, billing address and payment details in order to process the payment on our behalf.

7

Human resources data

Stere collects personal information from current, prospective and former employees and contractors, their emergency contacts, and beneficiaries under any insurance policy. We use this data only for employment-related purposes, including:

  • human resources administration and communication;
  • payroll, benefits and compensation administration;
  • recruitment, performance and development management;
  • absence management and employee safety;
  • IT support, regulatory compliance and internal or external audits;
  • litigation evaluation, prosecution and defence; and
  • processing of expenses and travel charges.

If Stere intends to use this information for any other purpose, we will notify the individual and provide an opportunity to opt out.

8

Sharing information

Vendors and service providers. We share information with vendors, service providers and contractors that support the Services or our business operations — including cloud hosting, IT services, payment processing, communications and customer support. Stere executes contracts with these providers that prohibit them from using or sharing personal information except as necessary to perform services on our behalf or to comply with law.

Affiliates. We may share personal information with our affiliates for internal business purposes or to provide a product or service you have requested, subject to written confidentiality and security commitments.

Clients. Where you interact with a program operated by one of our Clients, information relating to that program is available to the Client as the party responsible for it.

To protect Stere or others. We may access, preserve and disclose personal information and account content where we believe doing so is required or appropriate to comply with law enforcement or regulatory requests and legal process; respond to your requests; protect the rights, property or safety of you, us or others; enforce our policies or contracts; collect amounts owed; or prevent physical harm, financial loss, or suspected illegal activity. Server logs may be reviewed for security purposes, and log data containing IP addresses may be shared with law enforcement in connection with investigations of unauthorized activity.

Other third parties. We may disclose personal information to third parties you explicitly ask us to send it to, or about whom you are otherwise notified and give consent.

Merger, sale or other asset transfer. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing due diligence, reorganization, bankruptcy, receivership, sale of assets, or transition of service to another provider, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction as permitted by law and contract. Stere will endeavour to require the transferee to use personal information in a manner consistent with the Privacy Policy in effect when it was collected.

Stere does not sell personal information, and does not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

9

Your choices and opt-out rights

General

You may withdraw consent to our processing of your personal information at any time by contacting privacy@stere.io. Even after you opt out, we may continue to collect and use non-personal information about activity on our sites. You may review and change most of your personal information by signing in to the Platform, and you should promptly update it if it changes or is inaccurate. We retain personal information from closed accounts in order to comply with law, prevent fraud, collect fees owed, resolve disputes, assist with investigations and enforce our agreements.

Email and telephone communications

If you receive an unwanted marketing email from us, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the message. We will process your request within a reasonable time. You will continue to receive transaction-related and operational messages about services you have requested, as well as certain non-promotional communications — for example notice of updates to our terms or this Policy — which you cannot opt out of. We maintain “do-not-call” and “do-not-mail” lists as required by law and process requests within 60 days of receipt, or sooner where required.

Cookies

You can stop or restrict cookies on your device by adjusting your browser preferences. Cookie-based opt-outs must be performed on each browser and device you want opted out.

“Do Not Track”

Do Not Track (“DNT”) is a privacy preference some browsers offer. We do not currently respond to DNT signals or similar mechanisms.

10

Rights of access, rectification, erasure and restriction

You may ask whether Stere is processing personal information about you, request access to it, and ask that we correct, amend or delete it where it is inaccurate. Where permitted by applicable law, email privacy@stere.io to request access, portability, rectification or erasure. Please include your full name, the email address associated with your account, and a detailed description of your request. Requests are processed in line with local laws.

If you are a User whose data is processed on behalf of one of our Clients, the Client is the controller of that data. Please direct your request to the Client; where you contact us instead, we will forward your request to them.

Although Stere makes good-faith efforts to provide access, there are circumstances in which we may be unable to — for example where the information is subject to legal privilege, would compromise the privacy or legitimate rights of others, is commercially proprietary, or where the burden of providing access would be disproportionate to the privacy risk. If we restrict access, we will explain why and give you a contact point for further inquiries. To protect your privacy, we take commercially reasonable steps to verify your identity before granting access or making changes.

11

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as you use our Services, or as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected: to provide the Services, resolve disputes, establish legal defences, conduct audits, pursue legitimate business purposes, enforce our agreements and comply with applicable law — including insurance record-keeping requirements that may apply to a Client’s program.

12

How we protect your information

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of the information we hold. These include role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, network and application firewalls, logging and monitoring, and physical access controls. Stere is SOC 2 and ISO 27001:2022 certified.

The internet cannot be guaranteed to be 100% secure, and we cannot ensure or warrant the security of any information you provide to us. We do not accept liability for unintentional disclosure.

By using the Services or providing personal information to us, you agree that we may communicate with you electronically about security, privacy and administrative matters. If we learn of a security breach, we may notify you electronically by posting a notice on the Services or emailing you. You may have a legal right to receive that notice in writing.

To discuss our security program, procedures and policies, contact security@stere.io.

13

International users and data transfers

Personal information collected by Stere may be stored and processed in the United States and in other jurisdictions where we or our service providers operate, including in cloud infrastructure. Personal information may be accessible to law enforcement or other authorities pursuant to a lawful request. By providing information to Stere, you consent to storage in these locations.

Where we transfer personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland to a country that has not received an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards — including the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum — together with supplementary technical and organizational measures. A copy of the relevant safeguards is available on request from privacy@stere.io.

Disputes over privacy or the terms of this Policy are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and adjudication of any dispute arising in connection with Stere or the Services will proceed in accordance with the Site Terms of Use.

Privacy concerns

If you are a resident of the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland and have a privacy or data-use concern, contact privacy@stere.io. We will use best efforts to address your concern within one month of receipt. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

14

Children

The Services are directed to businesses and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16 years of age. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, a parent or guardian should contact privacy@stere.io so that we can remove it from our systems.

15

Contact and accountability

If you have questions about our privacy practices or this Policy, contact us at privacy@stere.io. We will address your concerns and attempt to resolve any privacy issue in a timely manner. You can also reach us through our contact page.

16

Changes to this Policy

Stere may modify or update this Policy from time to time, so please review this page periodically. You will be deemed to have accepted the updated Policy if you use the Services after it is posted. If you do not agree with any portion of the Policy then in effect, you must stop using the Services. If we change the Policy in a material way — for example if we intend to use personal information in a materially different manner — we will give Clients at least 30 days’ notice so there is time to evaluate the change.

17

Definitions

  • “Agent” means any third party that processes personal information on the instructions of, and solely for, Stere, or to which Stere discloses personal information for use on its behalf.
  • “Client” means an organization that subscribes to the Stere Platform under an Order or SOW.
  • “Employee” refers to any current, temporary, permanent, prospective or former employee, director, contractor, worker or retiree of Stere or its subsidiaries worldwide.
  • “Personal Information” is any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (an “Individual”).
  • “Process” or “Processing” means any operation performed on personal information, whether or not by automatic means — including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, alignment, restriction, erasure or destruction.
  • “User” means an individual — such as a broker, agent, applicant or policyholder — who interacts with a Client’s insurance program operated on the Stere Platform.
  • “Third Party” is any company, natural or legal person, public authority, agency or body other than the Individual, Stere or Stere’s Agents.
